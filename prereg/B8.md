## 1. Why

Every pre-registered wrong graph so far is a reversal, and every graph has been introduced as fact ("The causal structure of this world is:"). Two questions stay open:

1. **Trust in the assertion.** Study 3 (`prereg/B6.md`, `prereg/B6_LLAMA.md`) showed that a reversal that cuts every X -> Y path costs accuracy also without the instruction to use the graph (R1: -20.85 for the GPT-4.1 family, -16.33 for Llama 3.3 70B), and the path probes showed that the models read such graphs correctly. Whether they would still follow a graph they are told may contain errors has not been measured. `gpt-5.6-luna` follows 92.89% of path-cutting reversals on Study 1's questions (exploratory) but has never been tested on Study 3's design.
2. **Other wrong graphs.** On the exploratory `price400` draws (117 `ate` and `ett` questions, three GPT-4.1 models, both lexicons, instructed prompts), a deleted edge hurt where it removes every X -> Y path, -13.74 [-22.22 ; -5.63], and a spurious edge, which never removes a path, cost nothing measurable, +0.67 [-2.32 ; +3.86] (`scripts/analyze_edge_types.py`). These were found after the data were seen.

## 4. The unit and the groups

A **draw** is one (item, corruption type, dose k). Its harm is the corrupted graph's answer minus the correct graph's, under the same framing (`h_ni`: `_NI` minus `ORACLE_NI`; `h_u`: `_U` minus `ORACLE_U`), correct = 1, averaged over the family's models with both answers parsed. Groups are those of `scripts/analyze_answer_change.py` at this commit ("answer changed, path cut": the shown graph has no directed X -> Y path and implies an answer other than the label).

## 5. The five tests, in each family

| Test | Quantity | Prediction |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | mean `h_ni` over reversal draws in "answer changed, path cut" | negative: Study 3's R1 on fresh questions |
| T1 | mean `h_ni` over deleted-edge draws in "answer changed, path cut" | negative |
| T2 | mean `h_ni` over all spurious-edge draws | equivalent to zero within 5 points |
| T3 | mean `h_u` over the T0 draws | negative: the model still follows a graph it is told may contain errors |
| T4 | mean of `h_u - h_ni` over the T0 draws | positive: the doubt reduces the harm |

## 6. Decision rule

Bootstrap over items, 4,000 draws, every draw of an item resampled with it (`analyze_b6.boot_mean`). Two-sided p is `stats.boot_p`; T2 is two one-sided tests against -5 and +5, p the larger one-sided p (the 90% interval lying inside (-5, +5)). Holm across the five tests of a family, family-wise alpha 0.05. **Confirmed**: adjusted p < 0.05 in the predicted direction (T2: adjusted TOST p < 0.05); **contradicted**: adjusted p < 0.05 the other way; otherwise **not confirmed**. Seeds 20260907 (primary), 1, 2, 3, 4; a verdict that changes with the seed is reported as borderline. Sensitivity, no verdict: unparsed answers scored as wrong.

## 7. The analysis code is the exploratory construction

`scripts/analyze_b8.py` computes the five tests. Before it reads a B8 record it runs the T1 and T2 constructions on the exploratory `price400` draws and stops unless they return the published rows of `results/cladder/edge_types_harm.csv`. At this commit they do: T1 -13.74 [-22.22 ; -5.63], T2 +0.67 [-2.32 ; +3.86]. T0, T3 and T4 use the T1 construction on reversal draws. The replay check runs on exactly the listed draws.

Descriptive, no verdict: on the T0 draws, the share of answers that follow the graph (No) and the accuracy under each framing, and how often the answer equals the same model's answer without any graph (`RAW`); `ORACLE_U` minus `ORACLE_NI` accuracy (does doubt cost a correct graph anything); every test per GPT-4.1 model.

## 8. What will not be done

- No test is added or removed after the commit; anything else is exploratory.
- No data are replaced; a stopped run resumes from the cache.
- A family is tested only if every listed call of every one of its models has a record; a family whose run its cap or the provider stops is reported without verdicts.

## 9. Limits known in advance

- One question type pair (`ate`, `ett`), one prompt format, real names only.
- The GPT-4.1 arm has two models, not the three of Study 1 and Study 3, and both have the `shutdown_date` 2027-01-14 in OpenAI's model list, so the arm cannot be extended after that day.
- The doubt framing is one sentence; other wordings, or a stated error rate, may act differently.
- `gpt-5.6-luna` answers once per question at temperature 1.
- For `gpt-5.6-luna` and Llama, T1 and T2 have no exploratory counterpart.
